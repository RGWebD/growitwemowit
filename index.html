What we do

More than just mowing

Lawn Care Maintenance

Full lawn assessments and tailored plans designed with your unique yard and landscape in mind — mowing, edging, trimming and blow-off.

Hedge Maintenance

Frequent trimming keeps hedges and shrubbery healthy — removing dead or diseased growth so new growth can thrive and reducing insect problems.

Yard Debris Clean-up & Removal

Built-up pine straw, leaves, overgrowth on fences and trees that need uplifting — we clear the mess, even when the yard's a literal jungle.

Mulch & Pine Straw Installation

Mulch suppresses weeds, slows moisture loss and improves your soil over time — and it looks sharp doing it.

Flower Bed Clean-ups & Renovations

Bring tired flower beds back to life with clean-ups and renovations that boost your curb appeal.

Seasonal Maintenance

Single service or an ongoing maintenance contract — we keep your property looking right all year.
